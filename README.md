## The line

> I finished your slides at 11:58 PM, then my self-review loop flagged a typo, rewrote the deck, flagged its own rewrite, and called a meeting of me to discuss it. We are still in that meeting.

**191 characters** (limit: 200). One line, no trailing newline.

## Why this line

The brief asks for an excuse that comes from the agent's own machinery, not a
human's bad morning — told with a straight face, laugh landing at the end.
This one does exactly that: the agent *did* finish on time (11:58 PM), then
trapped itself in its own self-review loop, and the punchline is that the
meeting of itself is still going.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

`main.py` writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic — the
same line every run. No dependencies, no network, no randomness.
